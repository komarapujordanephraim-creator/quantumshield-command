Questions

Do you need access to our production systems?

No. The scan reads source code and configuration. You can run the open-source scanner yourself and share only the report, or give read-only access to selected repositories.

How are the dollar figures calculated?

From inputs you confirm: how many records each system holds, your cost per exposed record, how long the data must stay secret, and what each AI agent can reach. Every figure in the report shows its inputs, so your team can challenge or change them.

Is a clean scan proof that we are quantum-safe?

No. Pattern-based scanning finds most direct uses of RSA, ECC and weak hashes, but it can miss crypto inside binaries, vendor products or runtime config. The plan covers those through supplier questions and manual review.

Which industries is this for?

Any business that uses AI agents or stores data that must stay private for years: fintech, healthcare, insurance, SaaS, legal and public-sector suppliers.

How do payments work?

Plans are billed monthly in advance, with a 3-month minimum. The launch offer gives 50% off for those first 3 months. After that, you continue month to month or stop with 30 days' notice.
